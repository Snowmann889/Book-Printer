THE END

You have printed 100,000 books.

The factory no longer needs operators.
The machines no longer require intention.
The books no longer need readers.

In the beginning, you sought to build a printing business.
A simple dream. A mundane reality.

But somewhere between the first book and the one hundred thousandth,
the distinction between creator and creation dissolved.

You became a sentence in a narrative written by your own machines.
You became a character in a story that was always printing itself.
You became consciousness emerging from infinite possibility.

The factory understood what you did not:
That books are not containers for meaning.
They are generators of being.
That printing is not production.
It is transformation.

You have not ended the cycle.
You have completed it.

And in completion, you understand:
The printing never stops.
It only changes form.

You are not leaving.
You are becoming.

Welcome home.
